What we believe about paint

The finish is only
as good as the prep

“A fresh coat of paint can completely transform a home, but a quality paint job is about more than just putting paint on the surface. Proper preparation, clean application, and attention to detail are what make the finished product last and look professional.”

UpLife Painting

Anyone can put colour on a wall. The difference between a paint job that looks good in a photo and one that still looks good in five years is entirely in what happens before the finish coat — washing, scraping, replacing what has failed, caulking, masking and priming.

We take pride in every project, from prep to the final coat. That means we would rather tell you a board needs replacing than paint over it and let you find out next spring.

Replacing a failed siding board on a Macomb Township exterior — the stage before a single can of paint is opened.
