Cabinet, Door & Specialty Refinishing

Give kitchen cabinets, wood doors, basement wash tubs and other hard-to-match surfaces a fresh finish. UpLife Painting serves Macomb Township, Chesterfield, New Baltimore and nearby communities.

Schedule a Free Estimate Text Project Photos

Kitchen cabinets and vanities

Refresh cabinet doors, drawer fronts and face frames with a new paint color, or ask about stain for suitable wood. We clean, sand and prep the surface, then use a primer and finish suited to the material and existing coating.

For painted cabinets, we can spray doors and drawer fronts and roll cabinet boxes in place where the layout calls for it.

Wash tubs and utility sinks

Ask about refinishing your basement wash tub or laundry sink. We check the material, condition and how you use it before recommending a finish. Send a photo of the whole tub and a close-up of any worn or damaged spots.

Doors and other specialty surfaces

Ask about painting or staining doors, or updating built-in shelving and other painted woodwork. If you have a glossy, previously coated or unusual surface, tell us what it’s made of so we can assess the prep and finish during the estimate.
