Exterior Painting

Brick Home Exterior Project

How we do the work

  • We check siding, fascia and trim for loose paint, failed caulk and damaged boards before we start.
  • After washing and allowing the surface to dry, we mask windows and masonry, scrape loose paint and sand rough edges. Where repairs are included, we replace damaged boards before priming.
  • We spot-prime bare areas, use exterior paint suited to the surface and check coverage along edges, seams and trim before cleanup.

Interior and exterior painting, siding and trim, whole-house drywall installation and finishing, and power washing for homes across Macomb County, Michigan.
