Our exterior painting process

What happens
before we spray or roll

  • Inspect: Check peeling paint, chalking, loose caulk and damaged boards.
  • Wash: Clean suitable surfaces and allow proper drying time.
  • Repair: Discuss replacement of damaged siding or trim and prepare bare areas.
  • Mask: Protect brick, windows, doors, roofing details and landscaping.
  • Coat: Apply the agreed paint system according to the surface and conditions.
  • Walk through: Review coverage, edges and cleanup together.

Questions homeowners ask

Common exterior painting questions

Can you paint siding and wood trim on the same project?

Yes. We assess the existing materials, surface condition and coating needs before estimating the work.

Do you pressure wash before exterior painting?

Cleaning is often an important preparation step. We determine the right approach for the material and allow appropriate drying time before coating.

What if some siding or fascia is damaged?

We can evaluate suitable minor siding and trim repairs and explain whether replacement is needed before painting.

When is the best time for exterior painting in Michigan?

Temperature, moisture, drying time and the product manufacturer's requirements all matter. We assess conditions rather than promising a fixed painting season.
