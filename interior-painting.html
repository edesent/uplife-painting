How we approach an interior project

The steps behind
a cleaner finish

  • Assess: Review the rooms, surfaces, previous paint and any needed repairs.
  • Protect: Cover floors and furnishings and mask areas that should not be painted.
  • Prepare: Repair suitable defects, sand and prime where needed.
  • Paint: Apply the agreed finishes with attention to coverage and edges.
  • Review: Check the work with the homeowner and discuss any touch-ups.

Questions homeowners ask

Common interior painting questions

Do I need to empty the rooms before painting?

We discuss furniture and access during the estimate. Floors and nearby belongings need protection, and some items may need to be moved so the crew can work safely.

Can you paint the walls, ceilings and trim together?

Yes. We can review the full scope, including walls, ceilings, baseboards, interior doors and window trim, and quote the work based on its condition.

Will you fix wall imperfections first?

Minor holes, cracks and surface defects can often be repaired as part of preparation. We will identify repairs that need extra work before quoting.

What if I want to keep the same paint color?

Same-color repaints are common. We still inspect the walls, agree on paint and sheen, and determine whether priming or additional coats are necessary.
